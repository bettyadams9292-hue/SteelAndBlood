# Art Direction Bible

_Last updated 2026-11-04. Generated by tools/gen_docs.py from the real project state._

## Identity
Dark crusader fantasy meets undead apocalypse. Grounded 13th-century kit (mail, surcoats, great helms, heater shields) as the readable "hero" language; the world around it is ruined, fog-choked and lit by fire and a dying sun.

## Proportions & silhouette
Humanoids 1.80-1.90 m, slightly heroic shoulders. Silhouettes must read side-on at gameplay distance: helm shape, cape and weapon carry the read. Enemies get distinct postures (hunched walkers, low crawlers, top-heavy brutes) rather than recolours.

## Materials (Unreal: M_SB_Surface master + MI_* instances)
One master material with procedural grime (noise-driven, pre-skinned position on characters so it does not swim), micro-roughness variation, optional procedural chainmail ring pattern and emissive. Palette: Steel, SteelDark, Chainmail, Leather, ClothWhite, ClothRed, Gold, Visor, Wood, WoodDead, Stone, StoneDark, Iron, Ember, Bone, Flesh, Gore, Rags. Rule: never more than one hero-saturated colour (crusader red) per character; everything else desaturated.

## Lighting
Low warm key (dusk sun ~7 deg), cool moon rim from behind, warm practical fire light near the play lane, volumetric height fog for depth layering. Play lane always brighter than background.

## Camera
Side-on 2.5D, perspective CineCamera ~30 mm at ~7.6 m, play axis = world X, background layers at -Y, foreground framing only at screen edges.

## Composition layers
Foreground framing (rocks) -> play lane -> midground storytelling (graves, banners, braziers, litter) -> curtain wall/gate/towers -> far tower silhouettes.
